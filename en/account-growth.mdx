# Account Growth Guide

### Account Growth Best Practices

The Twitter algorithm has been publicly shared on GitHub. Based on the public information, we know:

The weight of comments in the algorithm has increased significantly.

Based on our tests, a well-growing account should meet at least the following:

(1) Post 2–3 tweets per day

(2) Maintain a higher volume of comments

(3) Respond promptly to replies under your own tweets.

Therefore we recommend:

Use the reply tool (extension) together with the Viral Library to achieve the best results.

- First, make sure your Context section is as complete as possible so ReplyBot can generate the most accurate replies and recommendations.
- Second, keep the ReplyBot time interval around 2 minutes to reduce the risk of being flagged as spam.

### Posting (Posts) Recommendations

Although ViralPosts provides many powerful creation templates, people today are often more willing to follow a more authentic you with less promotional content.

- We strongly recommend sharing some everyday life on X: small moments, photos, or a short daily update video.
