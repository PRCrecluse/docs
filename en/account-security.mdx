# Account Security

This section answers common questions regarding account security, ban risks, and spam risks.

## 1. API Security

Except for the ViralLibrary (a tweet database that does not involve critical account operations), we prioritize **official APIs** for operations whenever possible

## 2. Regarding Spam

- **Content Advice**: Adding your **Persona** to the **Context** will further improve the quality of your replies.
- **Risk Advice**: Follow our best practices and **avoid running the ReplyBot 24/7**. This effectively prevents you from being flagged as spam.
