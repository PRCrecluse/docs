# Account Security

This section answers common questions regarding account security, ban risks, and spam risks.

## 1. API Security

Except for the ViralLibrary (a tweet database that does not involve critical account operations), we prioritize **official APIs** for operations whenever possible

## 2. Regarding Spam

- **Content Advice**: Adding your **Persona** to the **Context** will further improve the quality of your replies.
- **Risk Advice**: Follow our best practices and **avoid running the ReplyBot 24/7**. This effectively prevents you from being flagged as spam.

## 3. Abuse & Compliance

1. We strictly prohibit account abuse. Do not use our platform to let multiple users operate the same X account at the same time, and we do not encourage sending large volumes of spam content in a single day. Our platform does not provide any bulk post publishing functionality. If your account is suspended due to behavior that violates X policies, we assume no responsibility.
2. We strictly follow X policies and prohibit any illegal activity or sensitive/forbidden content from being published through our platform. If detected, we will suspend the related account and you will bear all consequences.

- response to Twitter's increasingly stringent technical supervision.
- If you are concerned, we suggest you first test it on accounts with fewer than 500 followers.
- If you send illegal content in DMs or your product contains illegal content, your account may still be banned. We will not be held responsible in this case. Please be aware.

## 4. DM Sending Limits (Rate Limit)

- The following limits apply to both **SalesBot** and **AutoDM**.
- Based on X API user-level DM limits (15 messages / 15 minutes, 1,440 messages / 24 hours), we apply a stricter in-product throttle.
- Effective policy in Volumn.ai:
  - Minimum interval: **1 minute per DM** for each user account;
  - Daily cap: **200 DMs per 24 hours** for each user account.
- If a limit is hit, the task is not dropped. It will be rescheduled and continue at the next allowed time.
- We have implemented the necessary safety protections. However, if account restrictions or bans occur due to abuse (e.g., harassment/spam violations), we assume no responsibility.
