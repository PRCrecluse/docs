SalesBot is used for multi-round DM outreach and follow-ups based on your customer list, helping you run a structured sales conversation.

## 1. Core Workflow

### Create a Task

1. Select a customer list and create a task.

2. After the first round is sent, it enters the outreach stage (reached customers).
3. After the user replies, the system marks them as eligible for the second round.
4. After the second round is sent, it enters the follow-up stage (followed up).

## 2. Reach / Follow-up Definitions

We define “reach” and “follow-up” based on common sales scenarios and provide built-in metrics.

<Frame>
  ![Reach and Follow-up](/触达与跟进功能.png)
</Frame>

By comparing metrics across multiple periods and rounds, you can judge changes in product–market fit. The current-period metrics also show real conversion and purchase intent.

- The **first round** is called **reach**.
- A user is marked as:
  - **Reached**
  - **Needs follow-up**
  only after they reply to our first-round message.
- The **second round** is called **follow-up**:
  - After we complete the second-round reply, the user is marked as **Followed up**.

## 3. Metric Definitions

- **Current task total replies**: size of the current task list (target users for outreach).
- **Current task reached**: deduplicated users in the current task who were sent DMs successfully.
- **Follow-ups**: users who entered and completed the follow-up stage in the current task.
- **Total reached / Total follow-ups**: cumulative stats across all tasks under the account.

## 4. Sending Limits

To protect account safety and comply with platform limits, SalesBot and AutoDM share the following limits:

- Maximum 1 DM per minute;
- Maximum 200 DMs per 24 hours.

If a limit is hit, the task is automatically deferred; the platform is not responsible for account restrictions or bans caused by illegal abuse.
