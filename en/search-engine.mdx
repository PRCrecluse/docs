## Basic Flow

1. Step 1: open the “SearchEngine” tab.
2. Step 2: enter your product in the input box.

Keep the length between 30–100 words. If you enter too much text, the results may be abnormal (e.g., no customers found or errors).

<Frame>
  
</Frame>

Example: “I help founders grow their products on X.” (Volumn.ai example)

3. Step 3:

After about 5 minutes of processing, you will see the results:

Left side shows your target users, right side shows the sales leads you get.

4. Finally: view original users, view original leads, and export

(1) View original users:

Click any user in the left list to jump to the user’s X profile.

(2) View original leads:

The lead label is shown under the lead title on the right. Hover to preview the summary.

Click to jump to the original post.
