# [Must Read] Special Reminders

## Things We Don’t Recommend

### Setting your auto-reply frequency to once per minute

Replying once per minute may bring quick gains in the short term, but it can put your account health at risk and may attract increased platform attention. It can also burn through credits very quickly. At the moment, replying once every 2 minutes is the healthiest high-frequency interaction strategy.

## What We Want to Share With You

1. We are not in control of Twitter's algorithm, and we have no intention of changing the rules of the game. We simply believe that effort is naturally rewarded—whether through your account or in other ways.
2. Even if you use our product, we still sincerely recommend that you post more real, transparent, and genuine content, enjoy real life more, and create more authentic interactions. For example, share a few recent photos or record a short video to update everyone. Real experiences are what truly move people.
3. Wishing you all the best on your journey of account operation and growth. Have a wonderful life!
