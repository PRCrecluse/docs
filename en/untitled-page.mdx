## Common Use Cases

### 1. New Product Waitlist

When launching a new product and building a waitlist, many users line up under the original post. Sometimes we use “comment to join the waitlist” to get more engagement.

In this case, you can use the Audience Extractor, paste your original post link, and get all user contacts in one click.

It supports exporting to xlsx, and you can also use the list directly in SalesEngine.

### 2. Campaign

When you run Product Hunt pushes or community mutual‑support campaigns, commenters and retweeters often show high intent.

With the extractor tool, you can quickly pull those users and greatly improve outreach efficiency.
