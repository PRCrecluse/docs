# Viral Library Manual

Viral Library is your source of inspiration and content library, helping you quickly discover high-quality tweets and create content.

## 1. Recommended Content

In the **Recommended** section, you can see not only recommended **high-quality accounts** but also **trending tweets**.

> **Note**: Due to database limitations, the follower counts and post counts of recommended accounts may have a certain lag, but this does not affect the excellent quality of the tweet content in the Viral Library.

## 2. Save as Template

Click the **Save as template** button below any post to save it as your creation template.

- After saving, you can find the template in the **Queue** -> **Template** page.
- You can edit the template to make it fit your Persona style.

## 3. Calendar

The **Calendar** page allows you to manage scheduled tweets and customize your content calendar.

- Drag or click on a date to schedule posts.
- Intuitively view published and pending tweets.
